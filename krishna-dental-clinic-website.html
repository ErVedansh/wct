ABOUT THE CLINIC

Dentistry that takes time to explain itself

Krishna Dental Clinic was built around a simple idea: dental visits shouldn't feel rushed or intimidating. Dr. Payal A. Wankhede examines every patient carefully, talks through what's actually needed before recommending treatment, and keeps procedures as comfortable as possible — whether it's a child's first check-up or a root canal for a grandparent.

The clinic sits right on Alandi–Moshi Road near Rajmata Jijau College, making it an easy stop for families across Dudulgaon, Alandi, and Moshi.
